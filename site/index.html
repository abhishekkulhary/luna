Your cycle, beautifully private.

Luna is a calm period and ovulation tracker. Log your days, see your whole cycle at a glance and get gentle predictions — with no account, no cloud and no tracking.

Open the web app Download the app

Free · open source · works offline

01What it does

Your cycle as a ring

Every day of the current cycle, coloured by phase, with today marked. Predictions for your period, fertile window and ovulation adapt to your logged history.

Log a day in seconds

Flow, symptoms, mood, notes and intimacy in one sheet. The calendar shows logged days, predicted periods and your fertile window.

Insights that build up

Average cycle and period length, how regular your cycles are, and the symptoms you log most often.

Locked and encrypted

In the installed apps the database is encrypted on the device, with an optional PIN or biometric lock and reminders before each phase.

02Nothing leaves your device

Luna doesn't collect, transmit or sell any of your data. Everything is stored on your device, and you can export a backup file whenever you like. The web version keeps your data in this browser and cannot send reminders.

Predictions are estimates, not medical advice. Luna is not a medical device or a method of contraception.
