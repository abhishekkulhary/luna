# Privacy Policy for Luna

**Effective date:** 24 June 2026

Luna ("the app", "we", "us") is a period and ovulation tracking app developed by
**Abhishek Choudhary**. This policy explains how Luna handles your information.

**In short: Luna does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data. Everything you
enter stays on your device.**

## Information we collect

**None.** Luna has no account system, no server, and no analytics. We do not collect,
receive, or have access to any of your personal or health information.

All information you enter — including period dates, flow, symptoms, moods, notes,
temperature, and any other cycle data — is stored **locally on your device only**. It is
never uploaded, transmitted, shared, or sold.

## How your data is stored and protected

- Your data is stored in a local database on your device.
- On mobile and desktop, that database is **encrypted at rest** using a key kept in your
  device's secure storage (iOS/macOS Keychain, Android Keystore).
- You can optionally protect the app with a **PIN or biometric lock**.
- Because your data never leaves your device, only you — and anyone with access to your
  unlocked device — can see it.

## Device permissions

Luna may request the following permissions, all used locally on your device:

- **Notifications** — to remind you about your cycle and any reminders you set.
  Notifications are generated on your device; nothing is sent to us.
- **Biometric / device authentication** — only to unlock the app. Your biometric data is
  handled entirely by your operating system; Luna never sees or stores it.
- **File access (export / import)** — only when you choose to export a backup or import
  one. Backup files are written to the location you select (e.g. your Downloads folder)
  and are never sent anywhere by the app.

## Your control over your data

- **Export:** export all of your data as a JSON file to keep your own backup.
- **Import:** restore data from a backup file you choose.
- **Delete:** permanently delete all of your data at any time from Settings.

If you choose to share an exported file, that is your decision and your responsibility.

## Third parties

Luna contains **no advertising, no analytics, and no third‑party tracking**. We do not use
any third‑party services that receive your data.

## Children's privacy

Luna is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your
jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Because Luna
collects no data at all, no personal information from any user is collected.

## Medical disclaimer

Luna's predictions (period dates, fertile window, and ovulation) are estimates and may not
be accurate. Luna is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis,
or contraception guidance. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this page with
an updated effective date.
